Non-void elements:

In HTML, elements that require both an opening and a closing tag are known as non-void elements or container tags. Such include:

  • p
  • div
    • div doesn't change content on the page
  • ul
  • ol
  • li
  • h1, h2, h3, h4, h5, h6
  • header, footer, main:
    • main can be only one on the page
  • span
    • span is used to apply styles to a specific part of the text
  • mark
    • mark is used to highlight text
  • a
    • a is a tag that is used for inserting links, for example:
      • It's All Over Now, Baby Blue by Joan Baez
